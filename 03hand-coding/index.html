About

Why make this site?

Many people growing up don't always get a lot of experience helping in the kitchen, and when they move out and need learn how to cook themselves, it can be challenging. Famously, college students eat things like instant ramen, because it is cheap and easy, but it gets old fast. This site will teach you about ingredients, teqniques and how to use them to make better meals. A homecooked meal is always feels better than eating out, because you worked for it or someone who cares made it for you, I want everyone to be able to share that rewarding feeling.

How this helps you.

  1. Learn about culinary history, and understand how cooking has evolved over centuries.
  2. Understand the foundations of cooking and how to improve upon them to make more satisfiying meals in the kitchen.
  3. Know which utensils in the kitchen are important and worth spending money on.
