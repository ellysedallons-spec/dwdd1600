Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital citizenship is the idea of using technology—like the internet, social media, apps, and devices—in a safe, responsible, ethical, and respectful way.

What is Digital Law?

Digital law refers to the rules and laws that govern how people use technology, the internet, and digital content.

What is AUP?

An Acceptable Use Policy is a set of rules that explain how technology, internet, and digital resources are allowed to be used within an organization such as a school, workplace, or company.

What is Plagarisim?

Plagiarism is using someone else's work, ideas, or words without giving proper credit and presenting them as your own.

What is Academic Dishonesty?

Academic dishonesty is any action that gives a student an unfair academic advantage by breaking school rules about honesty and integrity.

What are the rules for AI Generated Content?

AI may be used only when allowed, must be disclosed if required, and cannot replace original student work.
